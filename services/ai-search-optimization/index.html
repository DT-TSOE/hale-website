AI Search Optimization

AI search isn't one channel. Each tool builds its own read on you.

Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews the same question and you'll often get four different answers, each built from a different read of who you are. Showing up "in AI" isn't one box to check. Every tool decides, on its own, whether to mention you at all.

Most brands have no idea how they land in any of them. Our job is to make the brand legible to all of them: clear category language, structured data, and the content depth that gives each tool something solid to cite instead of something vague to skip.

One honest note up front: none of this guarantees a citation. It removes the friction that keeps a brand from being citable in the first place. Hygiene, not magic.

Why this matters now

Buyers increasingly research brands, vendors, and products through AI tools before they ever land on a website. If an AI assistant can't confidently describe what a brand does, it won't recommend it, no matter how good the actual product or service is.

The foundation for this is mostly the same foundation strong SEO already requires: clear structure, real content, fast pages, and a site that says plainly what it does. AI visibility builds on top of that, it doesn't replace it.

How It Works

Entity clarity, structure, and content depth

Clear, consistent language about who the brand is and what it does, in the title, headings, and structured data, not buried in a tagline.

Content authority

AI visibility reporting

Where possible, tracking brand mentions across AI tools and monitoring whether new content actually gets surfaced.

How This Relates to SEO

AI search optimization doesn't replace SEO. It depends on it.

The sites that show up well in AI tools are almost always the sites with strong SEO fundamentals already in place: clean structure, real content, fast pages, clear crawlability. AI visibility is what happens when those fundamentals get extended with structured data and entity clarity on top.

If the SEO foundation is weak, AI visibility work won't have much to build on. If it's strong, this is the next layer.

AI search optimization FAQs

Does AI search optimization replace SEO?
No. It builds on the same foundation. A site with weak SEO won't get much benefit from AI-specific work until the basics are fixed first.
Can you guarantee we'll show up in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers?
No, and any agency that promises this isn't being straight with you. This work removes the friction that keeps a brand from being citable. It doesn't guarantee a citation.
How do you measure whether this is working?
Where tooling allows, we track AI-referred sessions and monitor whether the brand appears in AI answers for relevant queries over time. It's a newer measurement space, and we're honest about its limits.
What's the difference between AI search and traditional search?
Traditional search ranks pages. AI search summarizes and recommends based on how well it understands an entity. The inputs overlap, but the mechanism is different.
How long does this take to show any signal?
Longer than most people want to hear. Structured data and entity clarity are foundational work, and AI tools update their understanding on their own schedule, not ours.
